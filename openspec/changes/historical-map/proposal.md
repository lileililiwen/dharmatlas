# Change: Historical map exploration

## Why

Geography and movement are essential to explain Buddhist centers, routes, translation hubs, institutions, and archaeological sites in their historical context.

## What changes

- Add a time-filtered MapLibre map using OpenStreetMap-compatible data.
- Display places, institutions, routes, political regions, and archaeological sites with historical activity intervals.

## Non-goals

- No live navigation, modern travel planning, place reviews, or map editing.
