# Design: Contribution review and revisions

Contributors submit typed proposals against an existing or new entity. A reviewer can request changes, reject, or approve; only approval changes the published projection. Every decision records actor, time, reason, affected fields, and source references. Published revisions remain immutable and are linked in order so readers can inspect what changed and why. Conflicts such as overlapping dates or incompatible identities are surfaced for review rather than silently merged.
