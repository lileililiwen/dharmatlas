# Change: AI-assisted curation

## Why

AI can reduce the cost of extracting entities and detecting conflicts in scholarly material, but it must remain an auditable assistant rather than a historical authority.

## What changes

- Add human-reviewable AI drafts for entity extraction, relationship suggestions, source summaries, translation, name normalization, duplicate detection, and date-conflict detection.
- Record model, prompt/input reference, output, confidence, and reviewer decision.

## Non-goals

- No unsourced fact generation, invented citations, automatic publication, or autonomous historical interpretation.
