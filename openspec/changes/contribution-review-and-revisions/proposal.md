# Change: Contribution review and revisions

## Why

Dharmatlas is intended to be collaboratively maintained, but formal historical data must not be changed directly without review or lose its provenance.

## What changes

- Add contributor submissions for sources, dates, names, events, relationships, translations, and institutions.
- Add review decisions, publication gates, revision history, reasons, and rollback-safe record history.

## Non-goals

- No open direct editing, reputation system, chat, moderation forum, or automatic publication.
