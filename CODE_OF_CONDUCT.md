# Code of Conduct

Dharmatlas is a non-sectarian project. Contributors, reviewers, and
maintainers must not present one sectarian interpretation as uniquely
authoritative, and must not harass participants over religious affiliation,
tradition, lineage, or belief.

## Expected behavior

- Treat traditional accounts and scholarly claims as distinct, labeled
  interpretations.
- Cite sources for material historical claims; accept explicit uncertainty
  labels where evidence is thin.
- Review contributions on evidence and policy compliance, not on sectarian
  preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or demeaning language toward any person or tradition.
- Sectarian advocacy overriding source and review gates.
- Publishing another contributor's private data.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are
kept confidential. Maintainers will acknowledge receipt within 7 days,
investigate, and record the outcome. Consequences range from a warning to a
temporary or permanent ban from contribution and review queues.
